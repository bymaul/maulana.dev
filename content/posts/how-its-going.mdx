Hi, I'm Maulana Ahmad Aji Triadi, a web developer focused on building practical, efficient, and user-friendly digital products. My journey into technology started with curiosity about computers and how software works. I began experimenting with Visual Basic 6.0, eventually discovered web development, and started learning PHP, blogging, and web applications. Along the way, I also explored graphic design and participated in logo design competitions. In 2018, I joined a programming competition and placed second, which gave me more confidence to take programming seriously. I continued my studies at Amikom University Yogyakarta, where I built academic and personal projects while developing my understanding of software development.

After university, I started working professionally as a web developer and later took on freelance projects. I've worked across different parts of the stack, mainly with PHP, Laravel, JavaScript, TypeScript, React, REST APIs, and PostgreSQL. These experiences taught me that good software isn't just about making something work. It should also be maintainable, reliable, and useful. Freelancing also helped me become more independent, communicate better, and adapt to different projects, requirements, and codebases.

Today, I'm focused on growing as a full-stack developer and improving my skills in system design, performance, developer tooling, deployment, and scalable software. I'm also exploring technologies like Go and Next.js. Outside of professional work, I enjoy building personal projects to experiment with new ideas and learn new technologies. This website is part of that journey, a place to share what I'm building, what I'm learning, and how I'm growing as a developer. My journey started with curiosity, and that's still what drives me today: build something, break it, understand it, and make it better.
