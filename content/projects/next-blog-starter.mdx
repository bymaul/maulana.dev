Most of the work in setting up a blog is plumbing, not writing, and Next.js Blog Starter is the plumbing already assembled. Posts are plain `.mdx` files in a `posts` folder, so publishing one means adding a file, and there is no CMS and no database to run or maintain. It runs on Next.js 14 with the App Router, MDX for content, and Tailwind for styling, with syntax highlighting through `sugar-high` and nothing to configure. Dark mode, an about page, navigation, and a 404 are already wired up, along with `sitemap.ts`, `robots.ts`, a default OG image, and image optimization through `sharp`. TypeScript, ESLint, and Prettier are set up from the start. Clone it, point `config/site.ts` at your own domain, and start writing.
