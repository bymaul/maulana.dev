Curricula is a resume builder that runs in the browser, with nothing to sign up for and no server holding your CV. Data lives in IndexedDB with a `localStorage` fallback, and the app installs as a PWA, so editing and autosaving keep working with no connection. Three templates ship with it, Harvard, Modern, and Minimal, all tuned for A4 print output, with per-resume settings for accent colour, font, and line density, and export runs through your browser's own print engine. Local storage is the default; the AI features are the exception, and they send the CV to whichever provider is configured. They parse a pasted resume or a PDF, including scanned ones read with vision when there is no text layer, and rewrite a whole CV or a single section against a job description, showing a summary of the changes before anything is applied. Around that sit multiple resumes, undo and redo with in-session version history, custom sections, share links that carry the CV in the URL itself, and an interface in English or Bahasa Indonesia.
