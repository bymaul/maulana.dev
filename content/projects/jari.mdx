Jari puts Vim-style navigation in the browser. Scroll, tabs, history, link hints, find in page, and text selection all work from the home row, and every binding is editable from a settings page. Beyond the basics there is a visual mode with motions, text objects, and yank, screenshots of the page, a region, or the full page, a command palette, and a cheat sheet on `?`. Find in page handles regex, whole-word, and case toggles with its own history, the omnibox draws on your history and bookmarks, and tabs can be incognito, pinned, muted, duplicated, or moved to another window. Per-site control covers wildcards like `*.example.com`, alongside ignore and passthrough modes. It ships for Chrome, Edge, and Firefox, makes no network requests of its own, collects nothing, and is MIT licensed.
